# Surface material sources

These are generic scanned building materials, not Kaihua Temple surveys or historic mural photographs. Original 1K JPG files are stored unchanged. Saturation, tint, normal strength and tiling are adjusted only by the renderer. No geometry displacement is used on the walkable floor.

- Short Bricks Floor: https://polyhaven.com/a/short_bricks_floor
- Wood Table Worn: https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_worn
- Provider: Poly Haven and the artists credited on each asset page.
- Asset license: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Provider license statement: https://polyhaven.com/license

Included maps: Diffuse (sRGB color), nor_gl (linear OpenGL tangent-space normal), Rough (linear roughness).
